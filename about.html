About NovaCorp

Founded in 2015, NovaCorp Solutions has grown from a three-person consultancy to a 150-person firm serving clients across North America and Europe.

Our Story

NovaCorp was founded in 2015 by three former enterprise architects who saw a fundamental gap in the consulting market: too many firms sold strategy without execution, and too many implementation shops lacked strategic depth. We built NovaCorp to do both — and to do them exceptionally well.

Our first engagement was a database migration for a regional bank in Texas. Twelve weeks later, we'd cut their infrastructure costs by 40% and reduced query latency by 60%. That project became the template for how we work: small senior teams, deep technical ownership, measurable outcomes.

Today, our team includes former AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft engineers, CISSP-certified security professionals, and DevOps practitioners who have operated infrastructure at Fortune 100 scale. We've delivered over 200 enterprise migrations, built SOC programs for regulated industries, and helped platform teams ship faster than they thought possible.
