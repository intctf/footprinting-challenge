Cloud Services

Cloud Migration & Architecture

Whether you're lifting-and-shifting legacy workloads or re-architecting for cloud-native, our architects design landing zones that balance security, cost, and velocity.

We've migrated over 200 enterprise workloads across AWS, Azure, and GCP — including regulated workloads in healthcare, finance, and the public sector. Our average migration completes 23% under budget and 6 weeks ahead of schedule.

  • Migration readiness assessments & TCO modeling
  • Landing zone & network design (multi-account, multi-region)
  • Database migration (Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MongoDB)
  • FinOps & cost governance
  • Disaster recovery & business continuity
Security Services

Cybersecurity & Compliance

Offensive and defensive security services delivered by our certified team. From red team engagements to 24/7 SOC monitoring, we build defense-in-depth programs that stand up to real-world adversaries.

Our security practice is led by former red team leads from Fortune 50 banks and holds CISSP, OSCP, CISM, and GIAC certifications. We're also an authorized PCI-DSS QSA and SOC 2 auditor.

  • SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS readiness
  • Incident response & digital forensics
  • Security awareness training
DevOps Services
